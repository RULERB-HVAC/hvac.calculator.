# 🌬️ RULERB HVAC – Engineering Toolkit

**An integrated web-based toolkit for HVAC engineering calculations, technical references, and MEP design support.**

Designed & Developed by **RULERB + AI Agent**.

## 📖 Introduction

RULERB HVAC is a personal engineering project designed to support students, engineers, and people interested in Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC).

The platform combines engineering calculators, technical references, design guidelines, and practical utilities in one web-based application.

## 🚀 Main Features

### 1. Air Duct & Air Grille Calculator
- Duct sizing using the Equal Friction Method.
- Equivalent diameter and duct dimensions.
- Air velocity and friction loss calculations.
- Air grille sizing and effective free area.
- Design velocity recommendations.

### 2. Cooling Load Calculation
- Room-by-room cooling load estimation.
- ASHRAE Radiant Time Series (RTS) methodology.
- Solar radiation and Sol-Air calculations.
- Ventilation and infiltration loads.
- 24-hour cooling load profiles.

### 3. HVAC Unit Converter
- Engineering unit conversions.
- Power, airflow, pressure, and other HVAC parameters.

### 4. Chilled Water Pipe Sizer
- Chilled water flow rate calculations.
- Pipe diameter selection.
- Darcy-Weisbach pressure loss calculations.
- Colebrook-White friction factor calculations.

### 5. Hydraulics & Acoustics
- Airflow and friction analysis.
- Duct design velocity references.
- Air grille acoustic considerations.

### 6. Smoke Control & Fire Protection
- Basement smoke exhaust estimation.
- Corridor smoke exhaust calculations.
- Staircase pressurization estimation.

### 7. Engineering Resources
- HVAC technical references.
- Design guidelines and standard velocity tables.
- Ductwork and air grille image library.
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOP).
